Pasture Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts dry matter with multiplication and division: the grazable forage a pasture offers, the dry matter a herd demands, the days the pasture holds, and the rest a rotation gives. The acres, the yield, the herd and the rotation are yours to measure or read off your own grazing notes. It applies the counting as written, so it has not walked your fields and does not know your forage growth, your season, your species, your soil or your animals' condition, and it gives no verdict that a stocking is safe for the land or the herd. Every number here is your own entry.

The forage truth

Grazable forage: acres times yield times utilization.

The demand truth

Herd demand: head times weight times intake times days.

The days truth

Days the pasture holds: floor division, nothing rounded up.

The rest truth

Rest before the return: the paddocks not being grazed, times the graze.
